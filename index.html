The planet is drawn live, one pixel at a time, from a handful of numbers that change with the date: how molten the surface is, sea level, ice, haze, oxygen, plant cover and so on.

Dates and chapter facts follow the published record. The colours of the time scale are those of the International Chronostratigraphic Chart.

The continents are generated, so their outlines are not a map of the real ones. The Moon is drawn closer than true scale. Readings for the early Earth are mid-range estimates and carry wide error bars.

This page draws the planet with WebGL 2, which this browser has switched off or does not support.
