# rice

## Deep Time Globe

`index.html` is a single-file WebGL 2 toy that replays Earth's 4.54 billion years on a live, procedurally shaded planet: magma ocean, the Moon-forming impact and its debris disc, the first rain, the Great Oxidation, snowball glaciations, drifting supercontinents, greening land, mass extinctions and, in the last instant, city lights.

Open `index.html` in any modern browser. There is no build step and no dependencies beyond three Google Fonts.

- Drag to orbit, scroll or pinch to zoom, click the planet to drop an asteroid.
- Scrub either time scale, or step through the 18 chapters with the arrow keys. Space plays and pauses.
- The time scale uses the colours of the International Chronostratigraphic Chart.

The continents are generated, so their outlines are not a palaeomap, and the Moon is drawn closer than true scale.
